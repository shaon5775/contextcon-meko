# PM Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY): free LPG connection

Official portal: https://pmuy.gov.in
Compiled: November 2026. Always confirm current rules on the portal or at the LPG distributor.

## What it gives
- Deposit-free LPG connection in the woman's name.
- Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first refill and the stove (hotplate) are free.

## Who is eligible
- A woman aged 18 years or above, from a poor household (SECC 2011 list, SC/ST, PMAY-G, AAY, forest dwellers, tea-garden tribes, river-island residents, or any poor household submitting the deprivation declaration).
- **The household must not already have an LPG connection** from any oil marketing company (Indane, Bharat Gas, HP Gas). Existing LPG households cannot apply.

## Documents
- Aadhaar of the applicant (and family members), ration card or other proof of family composition, bank account details, passport photo, KYC form.
- **Migrant families:** a self-declaration of address is accepted instead of address proof.

## How to apply
- Online: pmuy.gov.in → Apply for New Connection → choose company (Indane / Bharat Gas / HP Gas) → fill form → submit documents to the chosen distributor.
- Offline: visit the nearest LPG distributor with the KYC form and documents.

## Help
- Helpline: 1800-266-6696 or 1906 (LPG emergency).
