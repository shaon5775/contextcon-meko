# Common problems across all Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) schemes

Checked against official sites: 9 Nov 2026. Compiled: November 2026.

## Aadhaar not seeded with bank account (NPCI mapper)
- Benefit money is paid to the bank account that is Aadhaar-seeded in the NPCI mapper.
- Fix: visit the bank branch and request Aadhaar seeding for DBT (consent form), or open an India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) account, which is Aadhaar-linked.

## Mobile number not linked to Aadhaar / OTP not coming
- OTP-based steps need the mobile number registered in Aadhaar.
- Fix: update the mobile number at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra / enrolment centre, or use biometric (fingerprint/iris) eKYC at a CSC.

## Name or date of birth mismatch
- Scheme records must match Aadhaar exactly.
- Fix: correct the scheme record to match Aadhaar (scheme portal or office). If Aadhaar itself is wrong, correct Aadhaar first with proof.

## Safety advice
- Government application forms are free. Never pay middlemen.
- Never share your Aadhaar OTP with anyone over the phone.
