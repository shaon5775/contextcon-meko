# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and the Ayushman Vay Vandana Card (senior citizens 70+)

Official portal: https://pmjay.gov.in (and the Ayushman App)
Compiled: November 2026, from the National Health Authority and public guides. Always confirm current rules on the portal.

## PM-JAY (general)
- Cashless hospital treatment up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year at empanelled hospitals.
- For poor and vulnerable families identified under SECC 2011 criteria (rural and urban), plus later additions.

## Ayushman Vay Vandana Card (launched 29 October 2024)
- **Eligibility: any Indian citizen aged 70 years or above.** Age is taken from Aadhaar. There is **no income limit**.
- Cover: up to Rs 5 lakh per year of free hospital treatment.
- Families already under PM-JAY get an additional Rs 5 lakh top-up for the 70+ member, not shared with younger family members.
- **CGHS / ECHS choice:** a senior on CGHS, ECHS or similar government health schemes must choose one. Take the Vay Vandana card and give up the other, or keep the other and skip Vay Vandana.

## How to apply
- **Ayushman App:** download → log in as Beneficiary → mobile number + captcha + OTP → enter Aadhaar → complete Aadhaar eKYC → card approved → download.
- **Web:** pmjay.gov.in / beneficiary portal → Aadhaar details → OTP → eKYC.
- **Offline:** any empanelled hospital or Common Service Centre (CSC).

## Documents
- Aadhaar card (proof of age is read from Aadhaar). Mobile number for OTP.

## Common problems
- Aadhaar shows a wrong date of birth (looks under 70): the date of birth must first be corrected in Aadhaar at an Aadhaar centre, with proof such as a birth certificate or passport.
