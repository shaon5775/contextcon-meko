# NSAP pensions: old age, widow and disability (central schemes)

Official portal: https://nsap.nic.in (also listed on https://www.myscheme.gov.in)
Checked against official sites: 9 Nov 2026. Compiled: November 2026. States add their own top-up, so the monthly amount differs by state. Always confirm with the local Block / Municipality social welfare office.

National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), Ministry of Rural Development, has five schemes, including:

## 1. Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)
- Age 60 years or above.
- Must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household as per the state BPL list.
- Central share: Rs 200 per month (age 60–79) and Rs 500 per month (age 80+); central amounts last revised in 2012. **States add more**, so the actual amount varies (for example, some states pay Rs 1,000 per month).
- Some states allow only one pension per family if husband and wife are both eligible.

## 2. Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)
- Widow aged 40 to 79 years, BPL household, not remarried.
- Central share Rs 300 per month, plus state top-up. At age 80 the widow moves to the old-age pension (IGNOAPS, Rs 500 central share).

## 3. Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS)
- Age 18 to 79 years, BPL household, disability of 80% or more.

## How to apply (general)
- Apply at the Block Development Office (rural) or Municipality office (urban), or through the state social-welfare portal / CSC.
- Documents usually needed: Aadhaar, age proof, BPL card or ration card, bank passbook (Aadhaar-seeded), photo. Widow pension: husband's death certificate. Disability pension: disability certificate.
- The application form is free. Do not pay middlemen.
