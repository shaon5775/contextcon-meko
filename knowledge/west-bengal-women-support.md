# West Bengal: monthly support for women (Lakshmir Bhandar → Annapurna Bhandar)

Official sources: West Bengal Women & Child Development and Social Welfare Department; socialsecurity.wb.gov.in; Duare Sarkar camps.
Checked against official sites: 9 Nov 2026. Compiled: November 2026. **This scheme changed in 2026. Always confirm the current rules at the block office, a Duare Sarkar camp, or the official portal.**

## Important change in 2026
- After the 2026 West Bengal Assembly elections, the state government notified the **Annapurna Bhandar** (Annapurna Yojana) scheme (notification dated 19/20 May 2026) to **replace Lakshmir Bhandar from 1 June 2026**.
- Benefit: Rs 3,000 per month to every eligible woman (same amount for all categories), paid by DBT into an Aadhaar-linked bank account.
- Eligibility as notified: woman resident of West Bengal, **aged 25 to 60**; not in permanent government employment, not drawing a government pension, not an income-tax payer. (Early news said the minimum age would drop to 21; the notified rules keep 25. A woman under 25 cannot apply yet.)
- Existing genuine Lakshmir Bhandar beneficiaries are moved to Annapurna Bhandar automatically and do not need a fresh application. Confirm locally.

## Old rules (Lakshmir Bhandar, for reference)
- Permanent female resident of West Bengal, aged 25 to 60, enrolled in Swasthya Sathi.
- Not drawing a regular salary or pension from a government or government-aided body.
- At age 60, women moved to the old-age pension.

## Advice for help-desk agents
- Do not quote old Lakshmir Bhandar amounts as current.
- Tell the citizen the scheme has changed and to confirm at the block office or Duare Sarkar camp.
- The form is free. Do not pay middlemen.
